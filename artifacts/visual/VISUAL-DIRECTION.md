# Blue Operations Board

White paper-like surfaces, navy ink/structure, cyan action accents. Narrative: chaos → align → control. Motifs are blue rails, operation numbers, proof windows and offset tabs. The telephone is the single generated metaphor.

## Hero decision
Variant A: agent_selected. The copy and overview stage coexist in the first desktop composition and preserve a straightforward mobile reading sequence. Variant B remains accessible at /design?variant=b; its centered type-first composition delays the product too much for this brief. Both have Arabic/English and 1440/390 captures. Product legibility assessment is pending real assets, not scored as passed.

## Foundations
Alexandria 400/500/700/800 and Space Grotesk 400/500/700, locally served via Fontsource with package licenses retained. No third font. Central tokens: src/styles/tokens.css. Borders 2px normal/3px emphasis; hard shadows 3/5/7px; no blur/glow decoration. Actual product colors will not be altered.

## Research use
The supplied sites serve as references for principles, not copied layouts. Prior read-only browsing opened Next.js official documentation, neobrutalism.com/docs, Feastables and GT Maru. No claim that all their interactions were tested. 21st contextual search returned cinematic scroll, CTA, dialog and form examples; none was imported because the project requires a restrained native scroll story and specific RTL compositions. Native components were authored for this project.

## Tool limitations
agent-browser failed to connect in this environment. Browser work continued through installed Playwright Chromium. Actual page screenshots were opened and evaluated. The generated image uses the built-in image generation tool, not an external paid provider.

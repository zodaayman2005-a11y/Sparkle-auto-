# Domain Pack — Ecommerce

## Primary outcome

Help shoppers discover, compare, trust, select, purchase, and recover from uncertainty with minimal friction and truthful merchandising.

## Dominant priorities

- Product discovery.
- Comparison.
- Trust and product truth.
- Variant and availability clarity.
- Price, delivery, returns, and total-cost transparency.
- Checkout completion.
- Mobile performance.
- Accessible forms and controls.

## Core journeys

- Browse/search.
- Filter/sort.
- Product detail.
- Variant selection.
- Add to cart.
- Cart editing.
- Checkout.
- Payment state.
- Confirmation.
- Return/support.

## Product presentation

- Use consistent image ratios and useful zoom/detail.
- Do not let lifestyle imagery hide product facts.
- Make variants, stock, dimensions, compatibility, and delivery explicit.
- Show price changes immediately.
- Label sponsored or promotional content honestly.

## Filters and comparison

- Preserve selections and result count.
- Make applied filters removable.
- Avoid hiding high-value filters.
- Provide meaningful empty-filter recovery.
- Comparison attributes follow purchase decisions, not data availability only.

## Checkout

- Ask for required information in a clear order.
- Show total, taxes/fees, delivery, and return terms before commitment.
- Preserve cart and entered data after recoverable errors.
- Give specific payment error recovery.
- Support guest checkout where product rules permit.

## Common failures

- Brand spectacle slows product discovery.
- Variant selected visually but not announced.
- Hidden delivery or fees.
- Filters reset unexpectedly.
- Product cards contain too much repeated detail.
- Checkout errors erase work.
- Mobile image carousel blocks key facts and actions.
- Fake reviews, scarcity, or urgency.

## Recommended skill order

`design-director` → `ux-architecture` → `reference-intelligence` → `component-system` → `visual-composition` → `typography-content` → `color-imagery` → `responsive-rtl` → `motion-interaction` → `design-to-code` → `visual-qa-refinement`

## Additional hard gates

- [ ] Price, availability, variant, delivery, and returns are truthful and clear.
- [ ] Cart and checkout preserve user state after errors.
- [ ] Filters and product comparison work with realistic inventory.
- [ ] Product images remain useful and performant on mobile.
- [ ] No fabricated review, scarcity, or urgency signal exists.

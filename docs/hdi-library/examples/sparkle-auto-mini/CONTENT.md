# Sparkle Auto — Content Contract

## Content hierarchy

1. Promise: control without physical presence.
2. Recognition: the owner’s operation depends on them instead of a clear system.
3. Definition: what Sparkle Auto actually is.
4. Fit: who it is designed for.
5. Method: operational principles and workflow.
6. Proof: real system screens and real capabilities.
7. Setup: how adoption works.
8. Commercial decision: packages and trial path.
9. Objections: FAQ.
10. Conversion: operational review request and WhatsApp.

## Locked Arabic messages

- Primary headline: «مش لازم تفضل في المغسلة عشان تكون مسيطر عليها.»
- Support: «بدل ما تسأل… افتح واعرف.»
- Tagline: «مغسلتك في جيبك.»
- Problem thought: «صاحب المغسلة مش المفروض يبقى سنترال.»
- Primary CTA family: «احجز مراجعة تشغيل» or the longer approved contextual variant.
- Secondary CTA: WhatsApp contact.

## Writing rules

- One main idea per section.
- Headline first; proof second; explanation third.
- Prefer concrete operational verbs: شوف، تابع، اعرف، جهّز، راجع، احجز.
- Avoid feature dumps before the problem and promise are clear.
- Avoid unexplained SaaS terms.
- Do not claim guaranteed revenue, total automation, or capabilities that are not verified.
- Arabic and English should carry the same intent; they do not need identical sentence structure or line wrapping.

## Forms

The qualification form may include:

- Name.
- Phone/WhatsApp.
- Car-wash name.
- Region.
- Number of branches.
- Approximate cars per day.
- Role.
- Current operating tools.
- Main problem.
- Best contact time.

Keep the first screen manageable. Explain why business details are requested and preserve mobile input ergonomics.

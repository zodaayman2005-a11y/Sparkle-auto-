# Sparkle Auto — Product Truth

- **Owner:** Hyzex
- **Primary market:** Egypt
- **Primary language:** Arabic
- **Secondary language:** English
- **Primary surface:** Marketing landing page for a real SaaS product
- **Related surfaces:** Owner/employee dashboard and customer booking web app

## Product definition

Sparkle Auto is an Arabic-first car-wash operations system. It helps owners and authorized employees manage day-to-day work and gives customers a self-booking web experience.

## Primary audience

Egyptian car-wash owners and operators, often 25–55, who may currently depend on WhatsApp, calls, notebooks, or spreadsheets. The interface must remain understandable for users who are not highly technical.

## Primary jobs

1. Know what is happening in the business without staying physically present.
2. See bookings, walk-ins, vehicle/service status, schedule, and daily activity.
3. Monitor money, customers, stock, staff, and branches according to permissions.
4. Let customers book services through a simple web flow.
5. Prepare the operation for more disciplined and scalable work.

## Verified product capabilities

- Dashboard and daily overview.
- Bookings and walk-ins.
- Schedule and service/vehicle status.
- Services, categories, branches, employees, and permissions.
- Customers, wallet, loyalty, offers, and ratings.
- Finance, reports, inventory, and operational notifications.
- Multi-branch support according to the selected plan and configuration.
- Customer self-booking web app.

## Locked positioning

- **Primary promise:** «مش لازم تفضل في المغسلة عشان تكون مسيطر عليها.»
- **Support thought:** «بدل ما تسأل… افتح واعرف.»
- **Tagline:** «مغسلتك في جيبك.»

## Conversion model

The primary landing-page action is requesting an operational review call. WhatsApp is the secondary contact path. A 14-day trial may be offered after reviewing operational fit and setup needs.

## Product-proof rules

- Use real Sparkle Auto screenshots when presenting the live product.
- Laptop product proof should prioritize a real overview/dashboard screen.
- Mobile product proof should use a relevant real booking or customer screen.
- AI-generated dashboards must never be presented as real Sparkle Auto UI.
- Do not invent integrations, automation, metrics, testimonials, or legal guarantees.
- Prototype data must be labeled and replaced before production approval.

## Primary success measures for the landing page

- A qualified owner understands the core promise quickly.
- The owner recognizes their operational problem before seeing feature lists.
- Product proof is legible and believable.
- The primary call request is easy on mobile.
- Arabic and English versions retain equal strategic clarity.

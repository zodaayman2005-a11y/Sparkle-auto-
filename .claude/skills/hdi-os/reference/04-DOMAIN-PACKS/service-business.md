# Domain Pack — Local and Service Business

## Primary outcome

Help a practical customer quickly understand the service, trust the operator, know whether it fits their location and need, and contact or book with low friction.

## Dominant priorities

- Plain-language relevance.
- Local trust.
- Service and eligibility clarity.
- Real photos or product evidence.
- Phone/WhatsApp/booking action.
- Price or quote process honesty.
- Mobile-first behavior.
- Accessible, low-friction forms.

## Audience considerations

- Technical comfort may be low.
- Users may scan on a phone while busy.
- Familiar words and clear next steps often outperform product jargon.
- Trust can come from location, process, real team, real results, response expectations, and clear policies.

## Content architecture

- What service is this?
- Who and where is it for?
- What problem does it solve?
- How does the process work?
- What is included or excluded?
- What proof exists?
- What does it cost or how is price determined?
- How quickly can the next step happen?
- How do I contact or book?

## Visual behavior

- Brand character can be bold, but service information stays direct.
- Use real environment and audience context.
- Avoid generic corporate stock photography.
- Keep contact actions persistent or easy to return to on mobile.
- Do not make the user decode an artistic metaphor before understanding the service.

## Forms and contact

- Prefer the smallest qualifying form.
- Match phone and WhatsApp expectations to real operations.
- State response time only when supported.
- Keep phone numbers directionally readable.
- Give clear success and fallback contact.

## Common failures

- SaaS jargon for a local service audience.
- Beautiful site with no service area or contact clarity.
- Fake team or stock images.
- Hidden pricing method.
- Long form before basic trust.
- Tiny phone/WhatsApp actions.
- Arabic copy translated from formal English instead of written for the audience.

## Recommended skill order

`design-director` → `ux-architecture` → `reference-intelligence` → `typography-content` → `visual-composition` → `color-imagery` → `responsive-rtl` → `motion-interaction` → `design-to-code` → `visual-qa-refinement`

## Additional hard gates

- [ ] Service, fit, area, and next step are immediately understandable.
- [ ] Contact and booking methods match real operations.
- [ ] Trust evidence is real.
- [ ] Arabic language/register fits the intended audience.
- [ ] Mobile users can act without navigating the full page.

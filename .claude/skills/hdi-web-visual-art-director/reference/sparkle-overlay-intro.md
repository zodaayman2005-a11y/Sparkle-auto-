PART II — OPTIONAL SPARKLE AUTO BRAND OVERLAY

Sparkle Auto — Web Art Direction Overlay

Role

Apply this brand contract while hdi-web-visual-art-director runs planning, actual
image generation, integration, and browser refinement. This overlay is not a separate
image tool and does not start a second competing production pipeline.

Scope: landing pages, marketing sites, product/campaign pages for Sparkle Auto.
The customer booking application and owner dashboard remain functional product
surfaces; do not force this marketing-page structure onto them.

Read order

Use this overlay only after the general hdi-web-visual-art-director skill is active.

When this consolidated skill is split into files, load only what the current task needs:

1. Sparkle Module 01 — Brand web contract.
2. Sparkle Module 02 — Section and asset plan when mapping a page.
3. Sparkle Module 03 — Production brief examples when producing a scene.
4. Sparkle Module 04 — Decisions and source coverage when resolving conflicts.

The overlay works from the textual Sparkle brand system, current project assets, real product proof,
and an internally established calibration anchor when recurring generated identity is needed.

The direction

Premium stylized 3D × Egyptian editorial thinking × fresh blue neo-brutalist web UI
× real Sparkle Auto product proof.

3D tells the story. Editorial composition shapes the idea. Web components provide
structure and action. Authentic product screens demonstrate the working product.
Whitespace keeps everything understandable.

Do not copy a social post into a webpage section. Do not create a generic blue SaaS
page with a mascot added. Do not generate the finished webpage as one picture.

Hard brand invariants

• Arabic/RTL first, English/LTR complete and independently composed.
• Owner identity from the locked Sparkle identity specification and accepted project anchor: same face, hair, facial hair,
proportions, navy overshirt, white T-shirt, beige trousers, navy shoes, dark watch.
Do not invent missing fine details or change them to fit a convenient pose.
• Premium stylized 3D; no photoreal owner, anime/2D redesign, or recognizable studio-character drift.
• Graphic palette: #01A2FF, #0C264B, #03132E, #91B1D8, #FFFFFF and controlled pale-blue derivations.
• Natural skin/hair and locked outfit colors remain correct; brand palette is not an
instruction to make skin blue or remove beige trousers.
• Navy outlines, controlled offset shadows, soft but not pill-everywhere corners,
strong readable typography, white/light surfaces, and deliberate empty space.
• One main idea and metaphor per story scene; the Owner acts inside the situation.
• Real product UI remains real. Do not pass screenshots through generation to restyle them.
• No false prices, features, notices, customer claims, or made-up operational metrics.

Product truth/accessibility/usability are hard gates, not lower-priority preferences.
Decorative inspiration cannot overrule them or quietly change the current project contract.

Production split

Generate: Owner acting, custom 3D props, car-wash-world scenes, and editorial
metaphors that the page actually needs. Use the actual available image tool; “an artist
must supply this later” is not the default when generation can do the requested work.

Build: text, header, CTA, pricing, forms, FAQ, outlines, shadow, layout, simple
spark/trail, numbers, diagrams, and product frames using real web components.

Use real: approved logo, verified product screenshots, approved product/brand assets,
and current commercial terms. Real proof must come from maintained product assets, not generated or illustrative marketing artwork.

Character rhythm

Concentrate Owner appearances in story moments: a supporting hero appearance if useful,
a strong problem scene, and a final relief/control scene. Trial/setup may use a small
prop rather than another Owner. Product showcase, pricing, FAQ, navigation, and footer
normally do not need the character.

A full page might use three strong character scenes instead of eight near-identical
poses. This is a recommendation, not a fixed quota. Do not put an arms-crossed/pointing
presenter next to every card.

Spark function

Every trail must have an action: lead the eye to proof, connect real journey steps,
show chaos becoming order, or hand off to the next section. If no sentence explains
its function, omit it. Prefer a lightweight SVG path over a heavy raster ribbon when
simple geometry can communicate the idea. Do not fill every gap with splashes/stars.

Conversion and source truth

Use the current review-first sales path unless the user explicitly changes it.
Default primary CTA: «احجز مراجعة تشغيل» / “Book an Operations Review.”
A trial is not instant self-service merely because a generic example says “start trial.”
Verify current trial eligibility and plan details before publishing.

Do not copy prices, car limits, dates, testimonials, or foreign-currency screens from
generated or illustrative visuals as current business truth. Locate the maintained product/commercial
source. Do not publish a success confirmation before actual successful submission.

Contrast correction

Opaque white on #01A2FF is approximately 2.76:1; navy #03132E on that blue is about
6.70:1 (calculation and source W1 in the general skill). Use navy text on the primary
blue surface, or an approved darker surface for white text. Do not silently darken
the main brand token to hide the issue. Check actual hover/focus/disabled states too.

Small blue-on-white text also needs correction. Keep bright blue for suitable surfaces,
accents, and appropriate pairings; use navy/dark blue for readable copy and links.

Responsive contract

Desktop design canvas 1440 px; preferred content max 1240 px; 12-column grid. Tablet 8
columns; mobile 4 columns with 390 px as the main art-direction review size. Support
narrow widths without horizontal overflow. Breakpoint tokens may follow the existing
project when compatible with these layout principles.

Recompose mobile. Keep promise, CTA, and real proof before nonessential illustration.
Avoid shrinking a whole laptop/phone/Owner collage into unreadability. Do not flip
character identity or product screens to localize.

Mandatory final checks

Brand without logo; one-second section meaning; real proof after hiding marketing
copy; readable Arabic/English; mobile thumb use; identity/alpha/physical contacts;
no repeated card template; keyboard/form states; reduced motion; measured delivery.

Use the general skill’s QA gates and the project’s stricter existing gates. Do not
claim a human-approved character or production-ready page without the corresponding
approval and evidence.

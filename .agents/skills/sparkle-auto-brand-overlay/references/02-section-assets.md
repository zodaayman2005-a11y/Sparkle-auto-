Illustrative production count

Start with one calibration asset. A likely small batch then includes the problem
scene and final scene, plus at most one or two useful props. Hero may reuse a suitable
asset or need no Owner. Desktop/mobile/direction variants are produced only when the
layout proves that they are necessary. This is not a fixed commitment to a number of calls.

RTL desktop starting composition

Hero copy can occupy the right 5–6 columns and proof/art the left 6–7. Product proof
should remain visually stronger than a supporting Owner. Owner gaze/action leads
back toward the message or product, never off-page. Keep the CTA within the copy flow.

Problem can open the composition: short headline above/right, dominant giant-phone
scene opposite, no cloud of six callouts. Product proof follows with a calmer large
screen so the page visibly moves from confusion to clarity.

Final CTA brings back confidence without repeating the exact hero pose. The action
is HTML in a stable high-contrast area, not attached to a moving raster element.

Mobile starting composition

At 390 px: concise eyebrow if useful, full readable headline, short support, primary
CTA, truthful focused proof, and an optional compact story image. Do not force the
entire laptop+phone+full-body Owner scene to fit one 340 px row.

Problem: keep the owner’s expression and phone relationship; shorten excess cable or
secondary edges rather than cutting the face/hand interaction. Use a dedicated mobile
scene if cropping destroys the metaphor. Pricing and form remain calm native content.

At 320/360 px verify wrapping, CTA reach, no side overflow, and actual screenshot
legibility. Mobile does not need decorative splashes from every desktop scene.

Work groups and checkpoints

A — truth + map: locate logo/screens, read current funnel/terms, resolve identity
specification/anchor, commit section map and first brief. Missing real proof remains explicit.

B — calibration: generate one expressive asset and compose the actual hero/problem
at desktop and mobile scale. Correct identity/light/crop now, before the rest of the batch.

C — assets + page: produce selected scenes, integrate real proof and reusable
components, keep omitted sections quiet. Preserve actual files/logs.

D — responsive + QA: language layouts, image derivatives, focus/contrast/states,
reduced motion, performance checks, screenshot review, and narrow fixes.

Do not automatically publish after group D. Release requires the user’s applicable
permission and closure of truth/quality blockers.

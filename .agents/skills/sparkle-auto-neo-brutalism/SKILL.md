# Sparkle Auto — White and Blue Neo-Brutalism
Authored adapter to master-plan §§2–5 and §12, not a new brand-system source.
Read PROJECT-CONTRACT and those sections before substantial visual implementation.
Pair with visual-composition, typography-content, color-imagery and the visual director.

## Visual thesis
Turn operational confusion into readable control. White space, confident typography,
navy frames and tangible hard shadows make the real product feel approachable.
Do not deliver a generic SaaS template with borders pasted over it.

## Tokens and readability
White #FFFFFF; Sparkle Blue #01A2FF; navy ink #03132E; dark blue #0C264B;
supporting blue #91B1D8; proposed pale-blue surface #F2FAFF.
Centralize them in the existing token system. Proposed values remain proposals until
selected. Keep true product screenshots, video and the authentic logo unaltered.
No yellow, orange, purple, green, rainbow or pure-black decorative palette.
Use navy on bright blue for essential text. The supplied source calculates white on
#01A2FF at about 2.76:1 and navy on it at about 6.70:1; check rendered states too.
Approximate direction: 2px border, 3px emphasis; hard 3/5/7px shadow offsets;
8/12/18/24px radii as roles, not random variations. No blurred glow or glass panels.

## Type
The plan proposes Alexandria for Arabic and Space Grotesk for English, subject to
the real project font source/license and visual testing. Do not silently replace an
already approved font. No font files are supplied by this package.
Test the real H1, paragraphs, mixed-script labels, prices and CTA at 390 and 1440,
then check 320/360. Do not letter-space or split Arabic letters for animation.
Compose line-height and measure separately per locale; do not enforce identical wraps.

## Composition and rhythm
One dominant focal point per section. The real overview screen should outweigh
decoration. Keep a focused booking view only when it remains useful and readable.
Alternate editorial text, large proof, story, steps and calm commercial content.
Avoid more than two consecutive sections in the same layout family by default.
Preserve meaningful content rather than hiding it to make a screenshot cleaner.
Build semantic text, buttons, frames, simple diagrams and accents in code.

## Asset execution
Use existing approved assets first. The plan proposes a small props-only art family:
a telephone metaphor in the problem and an orderly resolution at the end, with a
compact wash-bay prop only if useful. These are proposed briefs, not supplied images.
No mascot is mandatory. Do not fabricate product screens, logos or customer results.
Before authorized generation, define CODE/REAL/GENERATE/REUSE/OMIT, actual layout slots
and a calibration brief. Inspect the actual tool output and mobile crop before a batch.
Keep physically coupled objects coherent; verify transparency rather than assuming it.

## Interaction and mobile
Buttons compress their hard shadow with a small physical press; maintain clear focus.
Motion cannot postpone the H1, CTA or proof. Recompose small screens rather than
shrinking a poster. Do not mirror writing, real UI or asymmetric character identity.
If a screenshot is unreadable, enlarge it or use a documented truthful detail crop.

## Acceptance
Inspect hero + one strategy + one system screen together at desktop and mobile.
Check hierarchy, whitespace, Arabic glyphs, actual contrast and accessible focus.
Fix composition before regenerating artwork. Mark source gaps and agent selections.

Research is decision-driven: web-design-research → reference-intelligence →
inspiration-synthesis. Reuse the supplied reference dossier with its stated limits;
refresh a live/technical fact when it materially affects a decision. Do not rerun a
large research sprint for every button. Images are optional inspiration inputs, not
required uploads for the generic art skill.

Conditional only: competitive/trend intelligence for a real unresolved question;
creative-coding-lab for one bounded prototype; spatial/advanced experience for a
justified scene; data-visualization for real data; research synthesis for actual user
observations; instrumentation for an approved analytics system; experiments only after
baseline/traffic/permission. Installed is not activated.

Use one shared design/token owner and one scroll-engine owner. Independent content or
asset inventory can proceed in parallel, but do not have agents overwrite the same file.

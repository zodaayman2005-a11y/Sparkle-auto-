# Sparkle Auto — Bilingual Content and Conversion
Authored adapter to master-plan §§5, 8–11, 16 and appendices A/B.
Use content-information-design, localization-culturalization, responsive-rtl,
conversion-behavior-design and trust-privacy-security-ux only for the relevant slice.

## Source authority
Appendix A retains the supplied Arabic copy. Appendix B is a proposed English version,
not independently approved commercial policy. Section 8 contains the proposed answer
to the subscription-versus-purchase objection. Preserve terminology and supported detail.
Consult maintained repository sources before saying a price, limit or endpoint is missing.
Record source_draft, verified_in_repository, proposed, agent_selected and user_approved
separately. Archived mini-project examples are not current pricing evidence.

## Locale contract
Arabic/RTL first, English/LTR complete. Use the existing locale routing and data model;
do not create a second translation mechanism. Plan /ar and /en only if consistent with
the existing application. Localize controls, errors, loading/empty/success states,
captions, labels, alt text and metadata as well as headings.
Keep semantic reading/focus order coherent. Use logical CSS properties and isolate
mixed-direction numbers/phone/brand terms. Do not flip real product screens for English.
Label the actual screenshot/video language and do not invent translated media.

## Conversion
Keep the review-first CTA: «احجز مراجعة تشغيل» / “Book an Operations Review”.
Distinct secondary actions may show the real system; no equal competing trial/demo/
WhatsApp/review primaries. Do not turn a verified review-first trial into instant signup.
Use clear truthful value explanation, not fabricated scarcity, testimonials or counts.

## Subscription versus one-time purchase
Explain building cost, operation, maintenance, updates, support and specialization from
the supplied content. The plan flags the 3–4-year comparison and EGP 1,500 minimum as
unverified market claims. Keep them unverified unless a suitable source is supplied.
Do not promise every custom feature is free: the proposed text conflicts with the FAQ's
review/custom-work qualification. Keep the conflict recorded and publish only a resolved
maintained policy; do not silently adopt either as a new business commitment.
Do not invent the Expansion price or add a time period to the draft 600-car limit.

## Forms and privacy
Keep the supplied field requirements and verify them against current operation.
Visible labels; meaningful errors; preserved entries; loading; retry; double-submit
prevention. A success screen requires a real successful endpoint response. Local mocks
must remain visibly development-only. Do not send production test leads or customer
data without authorization. A missing endpoint blocks live submission, not UI work.
No analytics, consent guarantees, retention policy or WhatsApp number invented here.

## Public discoverability
Use existing domain, routes and deployment decisions for canonical/hreflang metadata.
Do not publish localhost canonicals or nonexistent structured-data claims. Keep public
content semantic and discoverable without needing to finish an animated sequence.

## Acceptance
Check source counts and section order using the master plan, not a generic template.
Review real 320/390/1440 layouts in both locales and test full form/FAQ/menu states.
Confirm every repeated commercial statement agrees or is explicitly blocked.
